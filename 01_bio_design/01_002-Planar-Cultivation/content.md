在籌備 Mycelium birdhouse workshop 過程中，我與博允在實驗室測試多種尺寸真菌複合材。圖中的材料大約為 A4 尺寸，我將它放在保鮮盒中，中間夾著原本用來瀝水的小隔板。圖中是為我們開箱時的樣子。複合材表面佈滿白色菌絲，在即將長成香菇時就放入烘箱烤乾，是最完美的採收黃金時間。

During the preparation for the Mycelium Birdhouse Workshop, Po-yun and I tested mushroom composite materials in various sizes in the lab.During the preparation for the Mycelium Birdhouse Workshop, Bojack and I tested mushroom composite materials in various sizes in the lab. The material shown in the photo is roughly A4-sized; I placed it in a food storage container with the small draining rack (originally used for draining water) sandwiched in the middle. The photo shows what it looked like when we opened the box. The surface of the composite was covered in white mycelium. Drying it in the oven just before it was about to grow into mushrooms is the perfect golden window for harvesting.
