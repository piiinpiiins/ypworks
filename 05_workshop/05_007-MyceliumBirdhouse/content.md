Mycelium Birdhouse - Living Materials and Machines Series vol.5

The theme of this mycelium workshop is the "Mycelium Birdhouse." Based on the dwelling forms of Taiwan's tree-cavity-nesting birds, we will use artificial shaping to cultivate mycelium material into a fungal enclosure sized for birds to inhabit. During the course, we will incorporate new techniques for blending mycelium bricks with other substrates, autoclave sterilization, and methods for making our own mycelium bags.

Course outline:
Week 1: Introduction to 1) common migratory birds in Taipei, and 2) mycelium samples
Week 2: Hands-on work with mycelium samples
Week 3: Mold exploration — experimenting with forms using molds developed for this course
Week 4: Lecture
Week 5: Results demo
